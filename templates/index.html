SAFETY FIRST

Know the common warning signs

Fraudsters often use urgency, fear, rewards, or fake payment support to pressure people.

🔑

OTP and PIN theft

Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, password, or card security code with anyone.

🔗

Suspicious links

Avoid unknown links and apps claiming to provide refunds, rewards, or urgent KYC updates.

📲

UPI payment requests

Review the recipient and amount. A UPI PIN is generally used to authorize a payment, not to receive money.

🎁

Fake rewards

Be cautious of unexpected prizes, cashback offers, jobs, or commissions that require an upfront payment.
